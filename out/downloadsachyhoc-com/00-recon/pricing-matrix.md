Source: `/goi-tai-khoan/` (fetched live, 2026-09-16). Prices in VND ("k" = ×1,000).
Promo banner: "Sale off lên đến 50% khi đăng ký thành viên" — valid until **20/09/2026**.

Business model: **membership / access tiers**, NOT à-la-carte book sales. A tier unlocks
download access to the library (~1,085 books). Each book is tagged with the minimum tier
required (free / Thường / VIP / GOLD / DIAMOND), shown as a "2nd note line" on the book info.
Books are WooCommerce **external** products priced 0₫; the real gate is login + membership.
